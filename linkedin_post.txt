🚀 **I’m thrilled to announce the launch of BatchForge!** 🚀

As a developer, I was tired of hunting down loose `.bat` and `.cmd` scripts scattered across my system. So, I built a solution: **BatchForge** — the ultimate Windows Desktop Automation Manager.

Built entirely from scratch, BatchForge gives you a centralized hub to write, organize, and safely execute your automation tasks. 

**Key Features I'm proud of:**
📂 **Categorized Library** – Instantly organize your scripts into a beautiful, searchable tree structure.
🛑 **Safe Execution Engine** – Run scripts asynchronously with real-time output. If a script runs out of control, our complete process-tree termination halts it safely.
🎨 **Syntax Highlighting** – A custom built-in editor specifically tuned for Windows Batch commands.
⏱️ **Task Scheduling** – Seamlessly bridges into the Windows Task Scheduler to automate backups and cleanups with zero friction.
🛒 **Built-In Script Collection** – Comes pre-loaded with over 30 incredibly useful, safe scripts ranging from system diagnostics to auto-extractors and focus modes. 

This project challenged me to dive deep into PyQt6, SQLite, asynchronous threading, and PyInstaller packaging. I’ve even built a custom setup wizard using NSIS!

Whether you are a sysadmin, a developer, or just someone looking to automate your daily PC tasks, I’d love for you to try it out.
