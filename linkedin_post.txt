🚀 **I’m thrilled to announce the official v1.0 release of BatchForge!** 🚀

As a developer, I was tired of hunting down loose `.bat` and `.cmd` scripts scattered across my system. Running them was always a leap of faith, and managing them was a nightmare. So, I built a solution: **BatchForge** — the ultimate Windows Desktop Automation Manager.

Built entirely from scratch in Python, BatchForge gives you a centralized, secure hub to write, organize, and safely execute your automation tasks. 

**Key Features I'm proud of in v1.0:**
📂 **Categorized Library** – Instantly organize your scripts into a beautiful, searchable tree structure.
🛑 **Safe Execution Engine** – Run scripts asynchronously with real-time output. If a script runs out of control, our complete process-tree termination halts it safely.
🎨 **Syntax Highlighting** – A custom built-in editor specifically tuned for Windows Batch commands.
⏱️ **Task Scheduling** – Seamlessly bridges into the Windows Task Scheduler to automate backups and cleanups with zero friction.
🛒 **Built-In Script Collection** – Comes pre-loaded with over 30 incredibly useful, safe scripts ranging from system diagnostics to auto-extractors and focus modes.
📚 **First-Run Tour & Learning Guides** – An interactive welcome wizard and a built-in PDF guide to help beginners master batch scripting on day one!

This project challenged me to dive deep into PyQt6, SQLite, asynchronous threading, application manifesting (UAC), and PyInstaller packaging. I’ve even built a custom setup wizard using NSIS!

Whether you are a sysadmin, a developer, or just someone looking to automate your daily PC tasks, I’d love for you to try it out.
